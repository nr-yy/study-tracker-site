Privacy Policy for Study Tracker

Last updated: 12 October 2026 Study Tracker is a study timer. It records how long you study, shows your progress, reminds you to start, and can block distracting apps while you work. This policy explains exactly what the app stores, where it goes, and what it never collects. It is written to match what the app actually does. Study Tracker is made by Study Tracker Team. For anything in this policy, contact studytrackermapp@gmail.com.

What the app collects

Your account

When you sign up, the app stores:

  • Your email address — used to sign in and to recover your account.
  • Your password — stored only as an encrypted form of it (a hash) by our authentication provider. Nobody, including us, can read your password.
  • A display name, if you choose to set one. Optional; your email is used if you don't.

Your study data

  • Subjects you create: the name, the icon and the colour.
  • Study sessions: which subject, when the session started, and how long it lasted.
  • Your membership status: whether your account is a paying member.

That is the complete list. The app does not collect your contacts, location, photos, files, or any identifier for advertising.

What stays on your phone and is never sent anywhere

Some settings are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • Which apps you have chosen to block.
  • Your reminder time and the days you plan to study.
  • Light or dark appearance.
  • A timer that is currently running.
  • Changes waiting to upload when you are offline.

None of this is uploaded, shared, or visible to us. Uninstalling the app removes it.

App blocking, and what it does not do

If you turn on app blocking, the app asks Android for two permissions:

  • Usage access ("see which app is open") — so it can notice when you open an app you chose to block.
  • Display over other apps — so it can show a "time to study" screen on top of that app.

**The app checks which app is in the foreground only to compare it against your own list.** That information is used immediately and then forgotten. It is never stored, never uploaded, and never shared. No record of which apps you use is created anywhere. Blocking runs only while a study session is active, or for up to one hour after a study reminder, and it stops as soon as you finish a session.

Notifications

With your permission, the app sends notifications to your device: a daily study reminder at the time you choose, and an alert when a countdown reaches zero. These are scheduled by your phone itself. No notification content is sent through any server.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase — provides the database and sign-in service that store your account and study data. Your data is kept in their hosted infrastructure and protected by access rules so that only your account can read or change your own rows.
  • Google Play — distributes the app, and (in future) processes subscription payments. Payment details are handled entirely by Google; the app never sees your card details.
  • Google Fonts — the app downloads its typeface from Google's font service the first time it is needed, and caches it. Google receives your device's IP address as part of that request, as with loading any web resource.

**There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs** beyond the above. The app does not profile you and does not sell or share your data with anyone.

How your data is protected

  • All communication with the server uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Database access rules mean each account can only read and write its own data, enforced by the server rather than the app.
  • Your membership status can only be changed by the server, never by the app on your phone.
  • Passwords are hashed by the authentication provider and are never stored in readable form.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • Deleting a subject immediately deletes that subject and all of its study sessions, on your device, on your other devices, and on the server. This cannot be undone.
  • Deleting your account removes your account and all of the study data attached to it. You can do this yourself in the app, under Profile → Settings → Delete account. It happens immediately and cannot be undone. If you can no longer sign in, email studytrackermapp@gmail.com from the address you signed up with instead, and it will be deleted within 30 days.
  • Signing out removes your data from that device, while leaving your account and data on the server so you can sign in again.

Children

Study Tracker is intended for students aged 13 and over, and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and it will be removed.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data held about you, correct it, or delete it. Email studytrackermapp@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
